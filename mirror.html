Who the mirror knows

Each person gets their own taste in each edition. Every painting is built from a subject, a place, a detail, the time, weather, light, colours, a style and a framing. In Bliss the bars show what each face lights up at; in Unease, what makes it tense up. Longer means a bigger reaction; fainter means it isn't sure yet. Everything is stored only on your laptop.

Moodscape

    
The Mirror

    
A painting that changes for whoever is looking at it. It recognizes you, and every few seconds it changes something: the place, the light, the weather, the colours, the style. It watches your face: what you warm to stays, what you cool on is undone. The longer you look, the more it becomes yours.

    
The painting is made live on your laptop. This phone sends it only numbers about your expression, about 14 times a second. No video or photos leave the phone.
